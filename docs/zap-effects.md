# Zap effects

Each battle stores `specialZapThresholdSats` in its existing Nostr session configuration. The default is 100,000 sats. A positive safe integer enables the effect at that amount **or above**; `null` disables it. The threshold checks a single receipt, not the player's accumulated score.

Admin → Zap effects provides the threshold, enable/disable checkbox and Preview all effects. The same controls appear in the display's Settings panel. Save applies configuration to the public display on its next session refresh. Invalid input disables saving.

Preview buttons cover the five existing tiers (1, 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 sats), the configured special amount and Time up. Choose PLAYER 1 or PLAYER 2 to inspect positioning. Previews reuse the production renderers and audio, auto-close and can be dismissed early. The preview sound checkbox shares the display Sound preference in this browser, persists in localStorage and synchronizes between mounted panels and tabs. Turning it off or closing/replacing the preview disconnects the preview audio channel. Unsupported or blocked audio shows a status message without blocking visual playback. They do not publish settings, initiate payments or modify scores. Previewing a regular tier deliberately plays that tier even if its amount exceeds a very low special threshold, so all designs remain inspectable.

Regular Zap effects share LEGEND's condensed typography and white/orange palette, increasing in size and confetti volume with each tier. They remain transparent overlays on the existing event background, with dark text outlines and shadows for readability on light colors and photos. On desktop they sit on the recipient's side, while Solo and narrow screens center them. Effect colors and fonts are independent of the event theme. The preview canvas uses the event's configured background color/image as well. LEGEND temporarily covers the entire display with its own opaque background, then reveals the event again without changing its settings.

Special receipts use a full-screen LEGEND ZAP treatment: an opaque black arena backdrop, condensed white/orange lettering, subtle floor lighting and a charging line, with a brief light impact, orange spark jets and falling confetti when the amount settles. Confetti uses the normal Zap palette and stays behind the content. The exact received amount reveals sequentially from units to the most significant place, alongside the recipient. Each digit cycles upward until it locks. Longer amounts get longer reveal times and smaller digits to remain within the viewport. After all digits settle, the exact amount stays fully visible for 6 seconds, then fades over 800 ms. The normal receipt celebration queue serializes effects; receipt-ID deduplication applies to both kinds. Starting/resetting a run clears pending effects.

The normal 10,000-sat tier is labeled MASSIVE ZAP. LEGEND ZAP has its own two-phase sound in both previews and live receipt playback: electronic ticks follow the rolling digits, then a bass impact, crash and sustained chord start when the last digit locks. Digits advance every 100 ms; the units lock after 900 ms and each remaining place locks 240 ms later (2.1 seconds for six digits). The sound uses the same digit timing as the visual, including longer amounts. The existing Sound toggle controls it; muting, resetting the run, leaving the display or closing/replacing a preview silences the pending impact as well.

Reduced-motion preference removes movement and immediately reveals the final digits; the special sound skips the rolling phase and plays the final impact immediately. Existing regular effects also use a static amount label under this preference.

Validation covers threshold boundaries, persistence/disable, exact right-to-left digit locking, normal tier selection, browser preview/save/reload, mobile/desktop rendering, receipt queue behavior and duplicate receipts. Browser fixtures intercept session/receipt responses; no actual Zap payment is performed.

## Time up and End

Time up uses a full-screen red finish treatment with TIME UP, 00:00 and a completion caption. After a 350 ms entrance it holds for 6 seconds and fades for 800 ms. Its audio starts when the queued visual starts. Both timer expiry and a transition to End trigger it, once per run; a later End after timer expiry does not repeat it. Opening an already ended battle does not replay the effect. The current Zap finishes first, then Time up takes priority over pending Zap effects. Public and preview renderers share the same duration.
